# ADDENDUM 01A: corrections to DOSSIER 01

**Wins over DOSSIER-01-alert-subscription.md and earlier addenda where they conflict.** Corrections and
measurements only. No member's position, vote or reasoning appears here.

Items are numbered on from the previous addendum so every correction has one stable number.
Verification row numbers (V1-V39) refer to VERIFICATION.md. Published 2026-03-02, after all six
Round 1 reports and before the tally.

## 1. Pilot take-up is one cohort, not the pilot

- **The dossier said:** 31% of the 2,380 pilot buyers took the paid plan (F7).
- **Correct:** 31% is cohort 1 only (189 of 610), the one cohort that also had a launch offer of
  three paid months at 1.00. By cohort: 31%, 18%, 14%, 11%. Pooled: 443 of 2,380 = 18.6%. The three
  cohorts without the offer: 254 of 1,770 = 14.4%. The dossier's 31% is withdrawn.
- **Source:** full pilot report, Dec 2025, cohort table (V6).
- **Found by:** all six seats flagged that the slide might show one point, not the series; the
  verifier supplied the cohort figures.
- **Matters because:** it is the main input to E1 and to question 2.

## 2. Churn figure is annual billing only

- **The dossier said:** monthly churn on the warranty plan is 1.1% (F8).
- **Correct:** 1.1% is for annually billed holders (36% of 9,400). Monthly-billed holders churn at
  3.8% a month; blended 2.8%. Option A would bill monthly.
- **Source:** billing system export, Jan 2026 (V7).
- **Found by:** cfo seat (first to say the monthly-billed rate was needed); also homeowner,
  landlord, insurer, platform and advocate seats; values from the verifier.
- **Matters because:** E1 and every payback figure built on it.

## 3. Platform price is per year, in a per-month column

- **The dossier said:** the platform bundle's alerts cost 29.99 in the "per month" column (section 2).
- **Correct:** 29.99 is a year on the annual plan (about 2.50 a month); the monthly plan is 2.99.
  Both are below option A's 3.50 a month. The table mixed a yearly and a monthly baseline.
- **Source:** marketing price sheet, Jan 2026 (V11).
- **Found by:** platform seat, which also noted the bundle price covers a home rather than one
  device; also cfo, homeowner, landlord and advocate seats. The "per home" basis is not stated in
  the price sheet and remains unchecked.
- **Matters because:** the competitive comparison and question 1.

## 4. Ireland and Netherlands rows are swapped

- **The dossier said:** 2025 units Ireland 18,900; Netherlands 11,200 (F3).
- **Correct:** Ireland 11,200; Netherlands 18,900. The total of 112,000 is unchanged.
- **Source:** 2025 sales ledger, country report (V4).
- **Found by:** verifier.
- **Matters because:** E4 used UK plus Ireland units (item 8).

## 5. Cost per device uses the year-end base

- **The dossier said:** about 1.75 per connected device (F5).
- **Correct:** 412,300 over the December base of 236,000 is 1.75, but a full year's cost divided
  by the year-end base understates cost per average device. The average 2025 base is not measured.
- **Source:** supplier invoices 2025; heartbeat log summary (V5).
- **Found by:** all six seats; confirmed by the verifier.
- **Matters because:** E5, option D, and every per-device cost comparison.

## 6. The 20% supplier rise applies to text messages only

- **The dossier said:** alert cost about 740,000 in 2027, at 2.10 per device (E5).
- **Correct:** the notified 20% rise is on text-message fallback, 65% of cost. On the dossier's own
  base: 1.75 x (0.35 + 0.65 x 1.2) = about 1.97 per device, about 690,000 in 2027. The 15% a year
  offline rate is unmeasured.
- **Source:** text-message supplier's price notice, Jan 2026; supplier invoices 2025 (V18).
- **Found by:** cfo, homeowner, landlord, insurer, platform and advocate seats independently.
- **Matters because:** option D and the size of the gap any option must close.

## 7. Option A revenue, corrected

- **The dossier said:** about 1.4 million annual recurring revenue at end 2027 (E1). Two Round 1
  reports put the end-2027 run rate at 0.95-1.1 million (cfo, advocate).
- **Correct:** at 3.8% monthly churn and a full year of sales: 0.72 million at 18.6% take-up, 0.56
  million at 14.4%. With a spring launch the end-2027 run rate is 0.53 million at 18.6% and 0.41
  million at 14.4%; cash billed in 2027 0.21 or 0.16 million. All before fees, running cost and build.
- **Source:** recomputed from items 1 and 2 (V14, V27). E1's timing point (a spring launch, not a
  full year) was found by the cfo, homeowner, platform and advocate seats.
- **Matters because:** question 2 and the payback of option A.

## 8. Option C sizing and the partner's terms

- **The dossier said:** about 160,000 a year from the partner (E4); the partner pays per
  policyholder "with an active sensor" (F12).
- **Correct:** with item 4, 75,500 x 8% x 24 = about 145,000; the partner's 8% share is stated for
  UK policies only, which gives about 123,000. E4 counts units sold, not policyholders with a
  working sensor. The letter says "active, alerting sensor" and sets three conditions the dossier
  left out: a quarterly alert-reliability report; monthly aggregated alert counts per policyholder,
  shared only with the customer's explicit consent; a review against claims after 12 months.
- **Source:** 2025 sales ledger; insurance partner letter of intent, Nov 2025 (V10, V17).
- **Found by:** insurer seat (units sold versus active policyholders; also cfo and homeowner
  seats); verifier (the swapped rows, the UK-only share and the three conditions).
- **Matters because:** question 4 and any bundle design.

## 9. The checkout effect was measured

- **The dossier said:** no measurement of whether showing the plan lowered purchases (E6, section 6).
- **Correct:** the full pilot report has one, though not a randomised test: checkout completion
  70.2% during the pilot against 73.9% in the four weeks before; 30-day returns 3.9% against 2.6%.
  The search had covered the summary slide only.
- **Source:** full pilot report, pages 4-5 (V19, V20).
- **Found by:** verifier.
- **Matters because:** E6 (unit sales under option A) and the 5% sales-loss limit in the goal.

## 10. Retail stock and notice terms

- **The dossier said:** stock cover about 14 weeks (F10); notice of changes to features or pricing (F11).
- **Correct:** 14 weeks is a unit-weighted average: 13 weeks at partner 1, 16 weeks at partner 2,
  longer than the 90-day notice. The notice clause also covers "packaging claims".
- **Source:** partner stock report, Feb 2026; partner contracts, clause 7 (V8, V9).
- **Found by:** verifier; homeowner and advocate seats had flagged that stock and notice overlap.
- **Matters because:** question 5 and the "No subscription." packs in the channel.

## 11. Corrections to Round 1 figures

- **Said:** ROUND1-platform section 3 prices a "29.99 a month" platform plan (1,204.64 and 1,239.64
  over three years) and quotes the packaging as "no hub, no subscription".
- **Correct:** no such plan exists; on the 2.99 monthly plan the figures are 232.64 and 267.64. The
  packs read "Free alerts to your phone. No subscription."; there is no hub claim.
- **Source:** marketing price sheet; packaging artwork file (V34, V35).
- **Found by:** verifier.
- **Matters because:** the competitive comparison; the packaging quote is not load-bearing.

## Disagreements recorded, not adjudicated

**2027 alert cost for option D.** The landlord seat puts it at about 692,000: the dossier's 1.75
per device with the rise on text only (ROUND1-landlord section 2). The insurer seat says the
per-device base itself is too low: on an estimated average 2025 base of about 190,000 devices,
2025 cost was about 2.2 a device (ROUND1-insurer section 2), which on the cfo seat's arithmetic
takes 2027 cost towards 850,000. The verifier reproduced both (V18, V30); both rest on the
unmeasured 15% a year offline rate. If the lower figure holds, the 2027 rise over 2025 is about
278,000; if the upper, about 438,000. Analysing the heartbeat log by month of sale would settle it.
