# ROUND 2: Competitor perspective, large smart-home platform archetype (platform)

**Vote:** C, conditional on push alerts staying free on every device and the paid tier being limited to text or call escalation, extra contacts and a portfolio view
**Changed from round 1:** no
**Confidence:** medium

## 1. Vote and reason

- C is my Round 1 position in the tally's words: price rise, free push, paid text as an extra, insurer alongside. This seat reasons as an archetype; it states no real company's plans.
- After item 3 the bundle's alerts cost 2.99 a month or 29.99 a year (fact, V11), both below A's 3.50. A platform in this position would likely answer A with "leak alerts included in your plan" and would not need to cut its price to do it (assumption).
- C keeps the one claim a bundle cannot undercut on price, a free leak alert to the phone, while charging for the cost driver: text is 65% of alert cost (fact, F5). B alone leaves that cost uncapped (ROUND1-cfo section 6).
- Question 2: text goes in the paid tier, but only if free push is reliable. A free alert that fails is a weak defence, and a platform would attack reliability instead of price (assumption). Question 3: no 180,000 build; cost the smaller tier build first. Question 4: per portfolio, since bundles tend to price per home (assumption; unchecked, item 3).
