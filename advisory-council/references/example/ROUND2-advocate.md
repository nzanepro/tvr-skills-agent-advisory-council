## 1. Vote and reason

- C is my Round 1 position (price rise plus an optional escalation tier, push free for every buyer). It keeps a remote leak alert for non-payers, so it clears my first red line; A (F6) and D's 2028 paywall do not.
- The addendum makes A weaker, not closer: pooled take-up 18.6%, 14.4% without the offer, falling 31/18/14/11% by cohort (fact, item 1); monthly churn 3.8% (fact, item 2); 30-day returns 3.9% against 2.6% during the pilot (fact, item 9, not randomised).
- Condition 1: the tier's price and contents are on the box, shelf label, listing and app store page before sale in every channel, with no free trial that rolls into a charge.
- Condition 2: any unit from a pack reading "Free alerts to your phone. No subscription." keeps its full alert set, text fallback included, for life, as an existing buyer does (F10, item 10). Partner 2 holds 16 weeks of stock against about 13 weeks of notice (fact, item 10).
- Condition 3 (new, from homeowner section 5): text fallback moves to the paid tier only after the Q3 missed-alert tickets are split by channel. If more than half are push failures text would have caught, a basic text fallback for leak alarms stays free. A paid tier lapsing on a failed card gets warnings and a grace period, and push never stops.
