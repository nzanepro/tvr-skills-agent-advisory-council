# ROUND 2: Small landlord with 5-20 units (landlord)

**Read:** ROUND1-TALLY.md, every ADDENDUM-01*, and all Round 1 reports.

**Vote:** C, conditional on the paid tier having a per-portfolio price of no more than 1 a unit a month for five or more units, with alerts to a letting agent or second contact included.
**Changed from round 1:** no
**Confidence:** medium

## 1. Vote and reason

- C is my Round 1 position in the tally's words: B's one-off rise, plus a paid tier that carries a per-portfolio price and agent alerts (my option E). A price rise costs me 6 a unit once; A costs 504 a year for 12 units (V24).
- Free push matters most to me in empty flats. It reaches my phone while the sensor is on the tenant's Wi-Fi; under A the unpaid sensor in a let flat is close to useless (ROUND1-landlord section 1, F6).
- Price test for the tier (estimate): 1 a unit a month is 12 a unit a year (144 for 12 units) against about 1.37 a device a year of text cost after the rise (F5, item 6), so it covers the channel it pays for about nine times.
- Text fallback (tally question 2): paid tier is acceptable to me, since my need is a second contact, not text. The homeowner seat's ticket split should decide it for everyone else.
- My blind spot, stated: five-plus orders are about 7,150 units, 6.4% of all units (estimate, F13, V24). The portfolio price should be a pricing rule inside C's tier (several homes, one account, one bill), not a separate build.
