# ROUND 2: Mainstream buyer, home owner (homeowner)

**Read:** ROUND1-TALLY.md, every ADDENDUM-01*, and all Round 1 reports.

**Vote:** C, conditional on the paid tier being shown with its full price before purchase in every channel, no free trial that turns into a charge, and a warning and grace period before any paid feature stops on a failed card.
**Changed from round 1:** no
**Confidence:** medium

## 1. Vote and reason

- C is what I asked for in Round 1: a phone alert for a leak without paying, the price rise on the box, and extras at no more than 2 a month. The corrections made A worse for a buyer, not better.
- The pilot already shows buyers reacting to meeting a fee after the sale: 30-day returns 3.9% against 2.6%, completion 70.2% against 73.9% (fact, ADDENDUM-01A item 9; not randomised). That breaks the goal's "returns no higher than 2025" before any other effect.
- Only 14.4% paid without a launch offer (fact, item 1). So under A about 85 of every 100 new buyers (estimate) would own a sensor that stays silent away from home, and most would find that out at setup or after the trial.
- A buyer comparing offers now sees A at 3.50 a month for one sensor against a whole-home plan at 2.99 a month or 29.99 a year (fact, item 3). A is the dearer subscription, not the simple one.
- Text fallback: keep it in the paid tier only until the Q3 missed-alert tickets are split by channel (tally question 2). If push is what fails, text belongs in the free tier and the tier sells call escalation and extra contacts instead.
